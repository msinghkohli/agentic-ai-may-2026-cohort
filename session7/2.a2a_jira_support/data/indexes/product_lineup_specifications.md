# **Orange Electronics: Product Lineup &** **Specifications**

**Company Motto:** "Fresh Thinking. Organic Design."

## **1. Smartphone Lineup: The Zest Series**


**Flagship: Zest Ultra -** The Zest Ultra is our premier flagship smartphone, designed for those
who demand the absolute best in mobile technology. It features a stunning 6.9-inch Lumina
OLED Display equipped with LTPO technology that adapts the refresh rate from 1Hz to 120Hz,
along with always-on capability. Powering this device is the industry-leading Orange O18 Pro
Chip, built on a 3nm architecture with a 6-core CPU, 6-core GPU, and a 16-core Neural
Engine. The camera system is a photographer's dream, boasting a 48MP Wide main lens with
an f/1.7 aperture, a 48MP Ultrawide lens with a 120° field of view, and a 12MP Telephoto
Periscope lens offering 5x Optical Zoom. The device is built to last with a 4800mAh battery
supporting JuiceCharge (35W wired, 20W wireless). Encased in a titanium alloy frame with a
Ceramic Shield glass front and textured matte glass back, it runs on ZestOS 18 and starts at
$1,199. "Pure Concentrate." Warranty: 1-Year Limited Warranty covering hardware defects and
malfunctions. Includes 90 days of complimentary technical support.


**Standard: Zest 18 -** The Zest 18 is a versatile standard smartphone designed for everyday
excellence. It sports a vibrant 6.1-inch **Lumina OLED** display (60Hz) and is powered by the
efficient **Orange O18 Chip** with a 4-core GPU. Photography enthusiasts will appreciate the
dual-lens system featuring a 48MP Main camera and a 12MP Ultrawide lens. The phone is
constructed from aerospace-grade aluminum with a color-infused glass back, available in
fresh colors like Tangerine, Midnight, Starlight, Mint, and Peach. Priced starting at $799, it truly
embodies the tagline "Squeeze the Day." **Warranty:** 1-Year Limited Warranty covering
hardware defects.

## **2. Computing Lineup: The Valencia Series**


**Laptop: Valencia Pro -** The Valencia Pro is a high-performance laptop engineered for
creative professionals. Available in 14-inch and 16-inch sizes, it features a **Liquid Crystal XDR**
(Mini-LED) display capable of 1000 nits of sustained brightness. Under the hood, users can
choose between the powerful **Orange M-Core Pro** and **M-Core Max** chips, supported by a
Unified Memory Architecture ranging from 18GB up to 128GB. Storage options are vast,
offering from 512GB up to an 8TB SSD. Connectivity is robust with 3x Thunderbolt 4 ports,
HDMI 2.1, an SDXC card slot, and MagLink charging. Starting at $1,599, it brings "Power to the
Rind." **Warranty:** 1-Year Limited Warranty. International coverage available for travelers.


**Laptop: Valencia Air -** The Valencia Air is an ultra-portable laptop that balances
performance with portability. Living up to its tagline "Light as a leaf," it comes in 13-inch and


15-inch sizes with a fanless, silent design that is just 11.5mm thin. Powered by the standard
**Orange M-Core** chip, it handles daily tasks with ease while offering an impressive battery life
of up to 18 hours of video playback. This sleek machine starts at $1,099. **Warranty:** 1-Year
Limited Warranty covering manufacturing defects.


**Desktop: Valencia Studio -** The Valencia Studio is a compact yet immensely powerful
desktop computer designed for professional video editors and 3D artists. It is driven by the
**Orange M-Core Ultra**, a chipset created by fusing two Max chips together for unparalleled
performance. Connectivity is a priority, featuring 6x Thunderbolt 4 ports and 10Gb Ethernet to
handle heavy data flows. This creative powerhouse starts at $1,999. **Warranty:** 1-Year Limited
Warranty with on-site repair options for enterprise clients.

$599. **Warranty:** 1-Year Limited Warranty covering hardware and battery (if under 80%
capacity).

## **9. The Vintage Orchard (Historical Products)**


_(No Longer Available - For Collectors & Nostalgia Only)_


**The Original Zest (2007) -** The device that planted the seed for the modern smartphone era.
With a revolutionary 3.5-inch multi-touch display and a single button interface, the Original
Zest combined a phone, a music player, and an internet communicator into one handheld
device. It introduced the world to "Swipe to Unlock" and the mobile web as we know it today,
changing how we interact with technology forever. _Status: Vintage._ **Warranty:** Expired.
Support discontinued.


**Pip Classic (2001) -** The music player that promised "1,000 songs in your pocket." The Pip
Classic featured the iconic mechanical "Scroll Ring" for navigation and a monochrome LCD
screen. It synced seamlessly with Valencia computers via FireWire, changing the music
industry forever with its distinct white earbuds and clean industrial design. _Status: Obsolete._
**Warranty:** Expired. Support discontinued.


**Valencia G3 "Pop" (1998) -** The computer that saved the company. The Valencia G3 "Pop"
was an all-in-one desktop that ditched the beige box for a translucent, colorful shell in
"Lagoon Blue." It was the first to omit the floppy drive in favor of USB, signaling a bold new
direction for personal computing and proving that tech could be beautiful. _Status: Obsolete._
**Warranty:** Expired. Support discontinued.


**Zest 4 (2010) -** Known for its "glass sandwich" design and stainless steel band, the Zest 4
introduced the high-resolution "Crystal Display," making pixels invisible to the human eye. It
set a new standard for industrial design in smartphones and introduced the front-facing
camera for "FaceChat" video calling, bringing people closer together than ever before. _Status:_
_Vintage._ **Warranty:** Expired. Support discontinued.


**Valencia PowerBook G4 (2001) -** The first wide-screen laptop encased in pure titanium. The
Valencia PowerBook G4 was an engineering marvel, just 1 inch thick, which redefined what a
portable professional computer could be. Its sleek, metallic aesthetic laid the groundwork for
the modern Valencia Pro line used by creators today. _Status: Obsolete._ **Warranty:** Expired.
Support discontinued.
